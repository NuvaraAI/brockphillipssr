Research & community planning

Research frameworks, source registers, manuscript drafts, and planning proposals connecting education, community needs, and technical organization.

My research and community work brings technical organization to questions that cross education, business, public services, and culture. The deliverables include frameworks, source registers, planning packets, manuscript drafts, and proposals.

My contribution

I selected questions, developed the direction, organized sources, and directed AI-assisted drafting and review. I sought recipient feedback and preserved corrections when a proposal did not address the actual need.

Research and planning artifacts

  • Coastal research: a public concept and evidence-accountability framework, source register, claim-status language, research agenda, and proposed comparative evaluation.
  • Memory research: an internal manuscript draft, literature references, and an evaluation plan. Proprietary details and the underlying conversation corpus remain private.
  • Community planning: delivered a planning packet, received substantive feedback, and offered a revision based on that feedback.
  • Education and business outreach: created pilot packets and no-cost workflow proposals for review.
  • Public-resource proposal: submitted a grant application for a free AI evidence kit; a receipt confirms submission.

Evidence and status

The reviewed records establish document creation, a delivered packet, feedback, outreach, and an application submission. They do not establish grant funding, an institutional partnership, restoration or housing outcomes, an accepted scientific paper, or demonstrated research efficacy.

The coastal agenda is concept development. The inspected pipeline reported no completed full manuscripts or accepted papers. The memory manuscript is an internal draft; proposed experiments are not completed experiments.

My paperback, The Crowned One, is the separately verified published work in this portfolio.

Skills demonstrated: research organization, source review, claim calibration, proposal writing, recipient feedback, document production, cross-domain communication.
