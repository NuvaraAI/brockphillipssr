## The work

I published *The Crowned One* under **Brock K. Phillips Sr.** The book examines sports culture, identity, admiration, mentorship, and responsibility. It extends my interest in human agency beyond software into cultural writing.

## My contribution

I provided the authorial direction and personal perspective, and directed AI-assisted editing and publication preparation. The project moved through manuscript organization, print preparation, cover work, review, and independent paperback publication.
